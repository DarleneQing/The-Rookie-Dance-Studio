## Design Context

### Users

The Rookie Dance Studio is a mobile-first web app for Zurich dance-studio administrators and members. Administrators manage courses, QR check-ins, subscriptions, payments, and student verification, often while operating the front desk. Adult and student members use the app to book classes, present their QR code, and understand their subscription and attendance status quickly on a phone.

### Brand Personality

Energetic, playful, and slightly futuristic. The experience should feel welcoming and youth-oriented without losing the clarity and trust needed by parents, adult members, and administrators.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a black-dominant “glow in the dark” visual system with softened near-white text and restrained cosmic-purple, lavender, and pink accents. Keep cards, dialogs, and large content panels near-black or subtly translucent; reserve opaque purple for intentional emphasis such as primary actions, selected states, small badges, borders, and decorative moments. Avoid large purple surface washes, low-contrast purple-on-purple combinations, generic card grids, and decorative gradients on routine interface text. Support both the brand’s playful sparkle/orbit motifs and a calm, legible working interface.

### Design Principles

1. Preserve hierarchy through dark neutral surfaces; color should indicate meaning or emphasis, not fill every container.
2. Optimize core booking, check-in, and account tasks for 360–430px screens and at least 44px touch targets.
3. Keep body text and critical status information comfortably readable at WCAG AA contrast, with visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support.
4. Use gradients, glow, sparkles, and rounded forms sparingly at memorable brand moments; keep operational screens clean and dependable.
5. Make the primary next action obvious within seconds and progressively disclose secondary detail.
